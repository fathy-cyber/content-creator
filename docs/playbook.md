STEP 1 — Strategy Module (Week 1)
What to Do First

text

Day 1 — Define Your Niche
─────────────────────────
Use the Strategy module to answer:

  WHO:    Which type of client do you serve?
          Example: "E-commerce brands doing $10K-$100K/month
                    that want to scale with paid social"

  WHAT:   What specific result do you deliver?
          Example: "We increase ROAS from 2x to 5x in 90 days"

  WHY US: Why you and not a freelancer or bigger agency?
          Example: "We only work with 5 clients at a time"

  NOT:    Who do you NOT serve?
          Example: "We don't work with service businesses or B2B"

Output: Save as a "Positioning Document" asset in the OS

text

Day 2 — Build Your ICP (Ideal Client Profile)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Use PersonaBuilder with YOUR client as the persona (not your client's customer):

  Demographics:   Industry, revenue range, team size, location
  Pain Points:    What keeps them up at night?
  Goals:          What do they desperately want?
  Objections:     Why would they NOT hire you?
  Triggers:       What makes them finally take action?
  Where they are: LinkedIn? Referrals? Google?

Output: Save as "Agency ICP" persona in the OS
        This feeds into all your own marketing content

text

Day 3 — Competitor Analysis
────────────────────────────
Use CompetitorAnalyzer on 3 direct competitors:

  For each competitor:
  1. Find their best-performing ad (Meta Ad Library is free)
  2. Paste ad copy into CompetitorAnalyzer
  3. Get: their angles, weaknesses, gaps
  4. Generate your counter-positioning

Output: "Competitive Landscape" asset
        Informs your brand voice and differentiators

text

Day 4 — Define Your Brand Voice
─────────────────────────────────
Use BrandVoiceService.extractBrandVoiceFromCopy():

  Input: 3-5 pieces of content you admire
         (can be competitors, can be brands you respect)
  Output: Extracted tone descriptors, forbidden words, style guide

  Then customize:
  - Tone: confident but not arrogant / direct but not cold
  - Forbidden words: "synergy", "leverage", "game-changing"
  - Value props: Speed, transparency, measurable results
  - Voice: Talk like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson

Output: Brand Voice saved in OS — now auto-injected into ALL future AI content

text

Day 5 — Set Your 90-Day Goal
──────────────────────────────
  Revenue goal: $X in 90 days
  Client goal:  N new clients
  Channel goal: Which 1-2 channels to focus on (not all of them)
  Content goal: How many pieces per week (be realistic: 3 not 30)

Output: "90-Day Strategy" document saved as asset
        Reference this before every campaign you create

STEP 2 — Business Module (Week 2)

text

Day 6 — Define Your Service Packages
──────────────────────────────────────
  Package 1 (Entry): What's the smallest thing you sell?
    Example: "Social Media Audit" — $500, one-time
    Purpose: Low-risk way for clients to try you

  Package 2 (Core): Your main recurring offer
    Example: "Full Social Management" — $2,500/month
    Includes: X posts/month, Y ads managed, monthly report

  Package 3 (Premium): Your highest-value offer
    Example: "Growth Partnership" — $5,000/month + % of results
    Includes: Everything + strategy + weekly calls

  Why 3 packages? Middle package gets chosen most.
  The premium makes the core look affordable.

text

Day 7 — Build Your Proposal Template
──────────────────────────────────────
  Use the Business module to create a reusable proposal structure:
  
  Section 1: Their situation (show you understood the problem)
  Section 2: The gap (cost of doing nothing)
  Section 3: Your approach (methodology, not just deliverables)
  Section 4: What they get (specific, tangible outputs)
  Section 5: Investment (your packages)
  Section 6: Next step (one clear CTA)
  
  Save as template → reuse for every new client
  Personalize only Section 1 and 2 per client

text

Day 8-10 — Financial Baseline
───────────────────────────────
  Monthly expenses (tools, software, contractors): $X
  Desired monthly profit: $Y
  Therefore minimum monthly revenue needed: $X + $Y
  Therefore minimum clients needed at core package: N

  This tells you: How many leads do you need per month?
  (Typical agency closes 20-30% of qualified leads)
  So if you need 3 clients: generate 10-15 qualified leads/month

STEP 3 — Marketing Module (Week 3)

text

Day 11 — Choose Your 2 Channels (not 5, not 1)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
For agencies and consultants, ranked by effectiveness:

  Tier 1 (Best for agencies):
  ✅ LinkedIn — organic content + DMs
     Best for: B2B clients, professional services
     Time: 30 min/day
     Results: 60-90 days
  
  ✅ Referral System — structured ask program
     Best for: Everyone
     Time: 15 min/day
     Results: 30-60 days (fastest ROI)

  Tier 2 (Good with budget):
  ⚡ Meta Ads — paid lead generation
     Best for: B2C clients, e-commerce brands
     Budget needed: minimum $1,000/month to test properly
     Results: 30-60 days

  Tier 3 (Good but slow):
  🕐 SEO / Blog — long-form content
     Best for: Building authority over time
     Results: 6-12 months minimum
     Good for: Passive inbound while doing Tier 1+2

  Pick 1 from Tier 1 + 1 from Tier 2 or 3.
  Do not spread across all channels simultaneously.

text

Day 12 — Build Your Campaign Structure
────────────────────────────────────────
Use CampaignManager to create your first campaign:

  Campaign name: "Q1 LinkedIn Lead Generation"
  Goal: 10 qualified discovery calls booked
  Budget: $0 (organic) or $X (paid)
  Duration: 90 days
  Channel: LinkedIn
  Target segment: Your ICP (link the persona you created)
  
  Success metric: Cost per qualified call booked
  (not followers, not likes — qualified calls)

text

Day 13-14 — Build Your Content Calendar
─────────────────────────────────────────
Use CampaignPlanner to map out 4 weeks of content:

  The 4 Content Types (rotate weekly):
  
  Week A — AUTHORITY content (40% of posts)
    Show your expertise. Teach something valuable.
    Example: "3 reasons your Facebook ads have low ROAS"
    Goal: Make people think "this person knows their stuff"

  Week B — SOCIAL PROOF content (30% of posts)
    Results, case studies, client wins, testimonials.
    Example: "Client went from 2x to 6x ROAS in 60 days"
    Goal: Make people think "this actually works"

  Week C — PERSONALITY content (20% of posts)
    Your opinion, your story, behind the scenes.
    Example: "I turned down a $10K client last week. Here's why."
    Goal: Make people think "I like this person"

  Week D — OFFER content (10% of posts)
    Direct invitation to work with you.
    Example: "We have 1 client spot open. Here's what we do."
    Goal: Convert warm audience to leads

  Save as CampaignPlan asset in the OS

STEP 4 — Content Module (Week 4 Onwards)

text

Now that strategy is set, content creation becomes fast because:
✅ Brand voice is already defined (auto-injected)
✅ ICP is clear (pre-fills audience fields)
✅ Campaign goal is clear (guides tone and CTA)
✅ Content calendar is mapped (no blank page syndrome)

Daily content creation becomes:
  1. Open PostGenerator
  2. Select platform + content type for today
  3. Enter topic (e.g. "ROAS improvement tips")
  4. Brand voice is auto-injected
  5. ICP context is auto-injected
  6. Generate → review → edit → save to library
  7. Submit for review (yourself or team)
  8. Schedule via SocialMediaManager
  Total time: 15-20 minutes per post

Question 2 — Which Workflows to Master?
The 5 Core Professional Workflows
Workflow 1 — The Lead Generation Machine

text

TRIGGER: Every Monday morning

FLOW:
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                │
│  1. ATTRACT (Content — 3 posts/week)                          │
│     PostGenerator → LinkedIn authority post                    │
│     Topic: solve one specific problem your ICP has            │
│     End with: soft CTA ("Follow for more" or "DM me X")       │
│                                                                │
│  2. CAPTURE (Landing Page)                                     │
│     LandingPageWriter → AIDA framework                        │
│     Offer: Free audit / Free strategy call / Lead magnet      │
│     Traffic: Organic post + optional small paid boost         │
│                                                                │
│  3. NURTURE (Email sequence)                                   │
│     EmailMarketing → 5-email welcome sequence                 │
│     Email 1: Deliver the lead magnet                          │
│     Email 2: Your story / why you do this                     │
│     Email 3: Case study / proof                               │
│     Email 4: Teach something valuable                         │
│     Email 5: Soft pitch for discovery call                    │
│                                                                │
│  4. QUALIFY (Lead scoring)                                     │
│     LeadService.scoreLead() runs automatically                │
│     Score > 70: auto-creates CRM contact                      │
│     Sales team gets notification                              │
│                                                                │
│  5. CONVERT (Discovery call → proposal)                        │
│     Use Business module proposal template                     │
│     Personalize Section 1+2 for this specific lead            │
│     Send via ClientReviewPortal for e-signature               │
│                                                                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

KPIs to track weekly:
- Posts published: target 3/week
- Profile visits from posts
- DMs / inquiries received
- Discovery calls booked
- Proposals sent
- Clients closed
